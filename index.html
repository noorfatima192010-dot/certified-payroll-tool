Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

The short version: what you type into the generator is saved in your own browser, and the free version never uploads it. If you turn on optional Pro cloud sync, your profiles are encrypted in your browser with a passphrase only you know before they are uploaded, so we cannot read them.

1. Who we are

Certified Payroll Generator (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a browser-based tool operated by Formify Tools. It helps contractors prepare weekly payroll sheets and statements of compliance. You can reach us at noorfatima192010@gmail.com.

2. Information that stays on your device

When you use the generator, the following is saved in your browser’s local storage on your own device:

  • Contractor and project information, remarks, and signer details
  • Prevailing wage rates you enter
  • Employee names, the last 4 digits of an SSN or ID number, withholding exemptions, work classifications, hours, pay rates, fringe amounts, and deductions
  • Pro users: saved profiles, payroll history totals, a record of your license activation, and, if you turn on cloud sync, your sync passphrase

PDFs are created inside your browser. Backup files you export are saved on your own device, and you are responsible for keeping them safe. Unless you turn on cloud sync (section 4), we do not receive, access, or store any of this.

The app only accepts the last 4 digits of an SSN or ID number. Please do not enter a full Social Security number anywhere in the tool. You can erase the saved form at any time with the “Clear saved data” button, or by clearing your browser’s site data. Because the data lives in your browser, it can be lost if you clear your browser data or switch devices, so keep your own records.

3. Information we may receive

  • When you buy Pro: payments, billing, and taxes are handled by our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record. We receive limited order information from it, such as your email address, order details, and license key. We never see your full card number.
  • When you contact us: your email address and the contents of your message.

4. Optional cloud sync (Pro)

Cloud sync is optional, off by default, and available only to Pro subscribers. If you turn it on:

  • Your profiles are encrypted in your browser before they leave your device. The encryption key is derived from a passphrase that you choose. Your passphrase is never sent to us. We store only the encrypted data, so we cannot read your profiles, employee names, SSN digits, or pay rates.
  • What we store: the encrypted data, a random value used in the encryption, a version number, the time of the last update, and a one-way hash of your license key that identifies your storage. We do not store your license key itself in this table.
  • What we receive when syncing: your license key and a device identifier, which we use to confirm with Lemon Squeezy that your subscription is active, plus standard connection information such as your IP address.
  • If you forget your passphrase, we cannot recover your synced data. Data stored on your devices is not affected.
  • Retention and deletion: synced data is kept while your subscription is active and for up to 12 months after your last sync, then deleted. You can delete it any time with “Delete my cloud data” in the Profiles window, or by emailing us.

5. Third-party services

  • Lemon Squeezy. When you activate Pro, and about once a week while Pro is active, the app sends your license key and a random device name to Lemon Squeezy to check that the license is valid. No payroll data is sent.
  • Supabase. If you use cloud sync, the encrypted data is stored with our database and function provider, Supabase, which also processes standard request information such as IP addresses.
  • Script providers. The page loads code libraries (for example Tailwind CSS and jsPDF) from content delivery networks. Like any website, those providers can see standard request information such as your IP address and browser type.
  • Hosting provider. Our host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested.

These providers have their own privacy policies.

6. Cookies and analytics

We do not set cookies, and we do not currently use advertising or analytics tools. The tool uses your browser’s local storage only to remember your work, your Pro license, and your sync settings. If we add analytics later, we will update this policy first.

7. How we use the information we receive

We use it to provide and support Pro, answer your questions, prevent misuse of license keys, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

8. Keeping and deleting information

Data on your device is yours to delete at any time. For information we hold, such as emails and order details, we keep it only as long as needed for support, accounting, and legal reasons. Synced data is handled as described in section 4. To ask us to delete anything we hold, email us.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

10. Security

Because your payroll entries are stored on your device, their security depends on your device. Please use a screen lock, avoid shared or public computers, choose a strong sync passphrase that you do not use elsewhere, and keep exported backups private. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

11. Children

This tool is built for business use and is not directed to anyone under 18.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest version.
